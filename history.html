HISTORY

The Legacy of OMPSA

Our Mother of Perpetual Succor Academy (OMPSA) was founded with a mission to provide quality, faith-centered education rooted in Catholic values. Since its establishment, OMPSA has grown into a nurturing environment for academic excellence, character formation, and community service.

With the guidance of the Blessed Virgin Mary, OMPSA continues to inspire generations of learners to become compassionate leaders and responsible citizens. Through the years, we have expanded our academic offerings, improved our facilities, and embraced modern technology to better serve our students and their families.

As we move forward, we remain steadfast in our commitment to holistic education—nurturing the mind, heart, and spirit of every child entrusted to our care.
